1. Introduction

This Privacy Notice explains how Modern Atelier (the team behind “ReinSense”, referred to here as “ReinSense”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) collects, uses, shares, and protects personal data when you use our sensor-equipped riding gloves, the paired ReinSense mobile application, related cloud services, and the website at getreinsense.com (together, the “Services”).

We handle your data in line with applicable data protection law, including the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the UK GDPR, the UAE Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL), and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA).

Questions about this Notice or how we use your data: info@getreinsense.com.

2. Who we are

The controller of personal data processed through the Services is Modern Atelier, the operator of ReinSense. Our company incorporation (ADGM, United Arab Emirates) is in progress; this Notice will be updated with the final registered entity name and address once complete.

Contact for privacy matters: info@getreinsense.com.

3. Data we collect

3.1 Data you provide

  • Account data: name, email address, password (stored hashed).
  • Profile data: riding discipline, experience level, goals, and horse details if you choose to enter them.
  • Communications: messages, feedback, and survey responses you send us.

3.2 Data collected automatically through your use of the Services

  • Motion data: movement data captured by the riding gloves during your sessions, and the riding insights we derive from it (such as your contact symmetry, hand stability, and ride score).
  • Session metadata: date, time, duration, device and sensor identifiers, calibration state.
  • Diagnostic data (opt-in): if you turn on “Send crash reports to ReinSense”, we collect crash and error reports and basic feature-usage signals to fix problems and improve the app. This is off unless you enable it, and it is limited to technical diagnostics tied to a non-identifying reference — never your name, email, or ride content.
  • Device data: model, OS version, app version, language, time zone, and a transient IP address (not stored beyond the request).

3.3 Audio

The audio coaching feature plays pre-recorded cues to your headset. The app does not record your voice or any environmental audio. Microphone permission is not requested.

3.4 Special-category data

We do not process special-category data as defined by GDPR Article 9. The motion data captured during your rides is used only to analyse your riding technique — it is not used to identify you, and we do not treat it as biometric data.

3.5 Children

The Services are intended for users aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under that age.

8. How we protect your data

  • Encryption in transit (TLS) and at rest.
  • Access on a need-to-know basis with audit logging.
  • Provider due-diligence at contract and renewal.

No system is perfectly secure. If a personal data breach affects you, we will notify you and the relevant authority where the law requires.

9. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you can: access your data; correct it; delete it (“right to be forgotten”); receive it in a portable format; restrict or object to certain processing; and withdraw consent where processing is based on consent. Most data is editable directly in the app, and you can:

  • Delete your account in Settings → Account → Delete ReinSense Account (erases your cloud data; your on-device rides stay on your phone until you uninstall).
  • Get a copy of your data in a machine-readable format — ask us through Help & Feedback in the app or at info@getreinsense.com, and we send it to you within one month.

To exercise any right, email info@getreinsense.com; we respond within 30 days. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your data protection authority (in the EU/UK, your national authority or the UK ICO).

California residents have the rights described under CCPA/CPRA, including to know, delete, and correct their personal information, and not to be discriminated against for exercising those rights. We do not sell or “share” personal information as those terms are defined under California law.

10. Automated decisions

We do not make decisions with legal or similarly significant effects about you using solely automated processing. Your post-ride reports are informational and are not used to make any consequential decision about you.

11. Changes to this Notice

We may update this Notice. We will communicate material changes by in-app notification and/or email before they take effect.
